Systems that let you stay out

  • Recipe cards by weight. Grams, oven temp and bake time for every cookie, so any trained staff member bakes the same cookie.
  • Opening and closing checklists. Signed daily by the shift lead.
  • POS with inventory. Daily sales, best sellers and stock alerts reach your phone every night.
  • Par levels for dough. Bake to a set number per hour of day to keep waste near your target.
  • Manager bonus tied to profit. A share of profit above break-even keeps them thinking like an owner.
  • Cameras and a weekly review. One hour a week comparing sales, waste and labour to this plan.
