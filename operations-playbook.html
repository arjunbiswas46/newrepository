Cookie Shop Operations Playbook

How a walk-in cookie store in Siliguri runs every day without the owner in it. Software records every bill, every UPI payment, every stock movement and every checklist. Staff follow written steps. You read one report a night on WhatsApp and spend about two hours a week on the business.

Targets match the Siliguri figures in the Cookie Shop Profit Planner: 250 cookies of 100 g a day at ₹60, costing ₹30 each to make, with break-even near 227 a day. Costs, rents and rules here are 2026 estimates; confirm them locally and with a CA before you sign anything.

Who does what

The shop has three layers. Each one only makes the decisions that belong to it.

Owner (you)

about 2 hrs / week
  • Read the nightly WhatsApp report (5 minutes)
  • Weekly review call with the manager
  • Approve spending above ₹5,000
  • Set prices, the menu and targets
  • Hire or fire the manager

Store manager

full time · about ₹25,000 / month
  • Writes the shift rota and weekly offs
  • Approves supplier orders up to ₹5,000
  • Trains staff and checks their work
  • Handles complaints and refunds
  • Owns the numbers in section 8

Shop staff

4 people · about ₹12,000 / month
  • Two shifts a day, two people each
  • Bake to the hourly plan
  • Serve customers and bill every sale
  • Complete opening and closing checklists
  • Log waste at the end of each day

Spending limit. The manager can spend up to ₹5,000 per order without asking. Anything larger comes to you on WhatsApp for a yes or no. This one rule removes most of the day-to-day calls to the owner.

The tool stack

Pick one tool for each job. The examples are widely used in India. Choose the billing app first, because most of the automation runs through it.

Billing app with stock tracking
Bills every sale, takes stock out by recipe, shows a UPI QR with the exact amount on each bill, and sends a daily report.
Petpooja, Restroworks (Posist), DotPe, or free Loyverse
UPI speaker
Reads out every payment, so staff cannot claim a payment that never arrived.

Recipes and baking plan

Anyone trained for five days should bake a cookie you cannot tell apart from yours. That needs recipes by weight, not by cups, and a fixed plan for how much to bake each hour.

Recipe card format

CLASSIC CHOC CHIP Batch: 32 cookies at 100 g dough each ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Butter, unsalted, soft 680 g Mix 3 min with sugars Brown sugar 540 g White sugar 270 g Eggs 4 (200 g) Add one at a time Vanilla essence 15 g Maida 900 g Mix on low until just combined Baking soda 10 g Salt 12 g Chocolate chips 720 g Fold in by hand ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── Scoop 100 g · Chill 24 h minimum · Bake 175 °C, 13–15 min Max 6 cookies per tray; they spread Done when: edges golden, centre still soft Photo of correct cookie: pinned above the oven Eggless version: separate card, separate tray, label "Eggless"

Make dough the day before and freeze scooped balls. Staff then only bake, which is faster and more consistent. Many customers in Siliguri ask for eggless, so keep an eggless recipe from day one and never bake it on the same tray without a clear label.

Stopping leaks

When the owner is away, money leaks through cash, payments to the wrong QR code, free cookies, missing stock and fake attendance. Each of these checks takes no one's time day to day.

  1. Only one UPI QR in the shop, the one printed on the bill by the billing app, with the UPI speaker next to it. Never a staff member's personal QR or a loose printed one.
  2. Every sale is billed. A sign at the counter says "No bill? Your cookie is free", so customers check for you.
  3. Each staff member has their own billing login. Cancellations and discounts carry a name.
  4. Blind cash count. Staff count the cash before the app shows what it should be.
  5. Weekly stock count of the five costliest items: butter, chocolate, eggs, boxes and nuts. The app compares actual stock with what sales say should be left.
  6. Selfie attendance at the shop location only, so no one is marked present from home.
  7. Staff eat free within a limit, for example two cookies a shift, logged as waste. No one needs to hide it.
  8. CCTV on the counter and the back door, checked only when a number looks wrong.

Hiring and training

Hiring

Hire for reliability and friendliness; baking can be taught. In Siliguri, staff who speak at least two of Bengali, Hindi and Nepali serve far more customers well. Keep the job post, interview questions and a paid two-hour trial shift the same every time, so the manager can hire without you.

Manager pay

Pay a fixed salary plus a monthly bonus of 10% of profit above break-even. This rewards selling more and wasting less. A bonus on sales alone would reward overstaffing and heavy discounts. Pay staff their Puja bonus before Durga Puja; it is expected in West Bengal, and it is already counted in the Profit Planner.

Choosing the shop

Look for walking crowds, not only traffic. Busy stretches include Sevoke Road, Hill Cart Road, Bidhan Road near Bidhan Market, and the area around City Centre at Matigara. Near colleges and bus or rail terminals brings students and travellers. Count people walking past for an hour on a weekday evening and on a Sunday before you sign.

Suppliers

Buy flour, sugar and packaging in bulk from the Khalpara and Mahabirsthan wholesale markets or a bakery-supply distributor. Get butter from a dairy distributor on a weekly standing order. Keep two suppliers for butter and chocolate, the two items that most affect cost and taste.

90 days to step back

You cannot be absent on day one. Build the system while you are still in the shop, then hand it over in stages.

Before opening
Get the licences. Set up the billing app, stock items and recipes in it. Write recipe cards and checklists. Hire the manager first and have them help hire staff.
  • Done when: a trial day runs with every bill, attendance and checklist recorded.
Days 1–30
You work in the shop alongside the manager. Fix every step that confuses staff. Turn on the nightly report and alerts.
  • Done when: the manager runs a full day while you only watch.
Days 31–60
You are in the shop two days a week. The manager makes the rota, approves orders and trains new staff. Set the hourly baking plan from real sales.
  • Done when: two weeks with no red numbers and no calls to you.
Days 61–90
You stop scheduled visits. Keep only the nightly report, the Monday call and the monthly review. Start the manager bonus.
  • Done when: a month closes at or above your target profit without you in the shop.
